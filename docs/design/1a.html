Total funds rose £223,700 over the nine months to 30 June, driven by a strong legacy quarter (£210,000 received) and steady Epworth income. Unrestricted free reserves stand at £2.93m — 10.2 months of budgeted expenditure, comfortably above the trustees' six-month policy floor.
Four funds carry flags this period. Chapel Buildings & Fabric spend is running 41% ahead of its trailing average following the Ullet Road roof works; the Youth & Young Adults Fund shows a £9,400 in-year deficit and will draw on brought-forward balances. Neither breaches policy limits, and both are itemised on page 6.
