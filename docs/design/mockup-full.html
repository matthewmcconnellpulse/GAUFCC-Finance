Total funds rose £223,700 over the nine months to 30 June, driven by a strong legacy quarter — £210,000 received against £48,000 in the comparable period — and steady Epworth income. Unrestricted free reserves stand at £2.93m, being 10.2 months of budgeted expenditure against the trustees' six-month policy floor.

The Epworth portfolio returned +2.1% for the year to date, with £52,180 of net gains recognised across the holding funds. Every transaction in the quarter was fund-coded at source, up from 91% in the first quarter.
